Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Welcome to cosmopings! This Privacy Policy explains how cosmopings handles information when you use our Discord bot.

cosmopings is a Discord bot designed to send notifications and reminders about scheduled livestreams, premieres, and cover releases.

1. Information We Collect

cosmopings processes and stores information needed to schedule and deliver notifications. Depending on how the bot is configured and used, this may include:

  • Video information: YouTube video IDs associated with livestreams, premieres, or cover releases.
  • Schedule information: Dates and times associated with scheduled notifications, including times stored in UTC (Coordinated Universal Time).
  • Discord channel IDs: Identifiers used to determine which Discord channel should receive a notification.
  • Notification records: Video IDs and related status information used to track notifications that have already been sent and prevent duplicate announcements.

The bot may store scheduling and notification records in local JSON files, including scheduled.json and posted.json.

cosmopings may access publicly available video or premiere information to schedule notifications. The bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment details, or private messages.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate cosmopings' notification features, including:

  • Tracking scheduled livestreams, premieres, and cover releases.
  • Determining when notifications should be sent.
  • Sending announcements to the configured Discord channels.
  • Preventing duplicate notifications.
  • Maintaining and updating scheduling records.
  • Troubleshooting and maintaining the bot when necessary.

cosmopings does not sell personal information or use the information described in this policy for targeted advertising.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

cosmopings uses Discord to deliver messages and may use YouTube or other relevant services to obtain publicly available video and scheduling information.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Please review the relevant policies of Discord and Google/YouTube for more information about their data practices.

  • Discord Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Scheduling information and notification records may be retained for as long as they are needed to operate the bot and prevent duplicate notifications.

These records may be updated, removed, or reset during normal maintenance or when they are no longer needed.

If you have questions about information handled by cosmopings or wish to request the deletion of information associated with your use of the bot, please contact the developer using the contact details below.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information used by cosmopings. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

cosmopings is a general-purpose Discord notification bot and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should use Discord and the bot in accordance with applicable age requirements and Discord's own terms.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if cosmopings' features or information-handling practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
